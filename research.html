Research

My academic interests lie primarily in stochastic calculus, functional Itô calculus, path-dependent processes and optimization.

Functional Itô Calculus

My work in stochastic calculus concerns functionals that depend not only on the current state of a stochastic process, but also on its past trajectory.

In particular, I am interested in the framework of Functional Itô Calculus and its applications to path-dependent stochastic systems.

Optimization

I am also interested in smooth optimization and the convergence analysis of iterative algorithms.

Current interests include ellipsoid-center methods, accelerated optimization and momentum-based algorithms.

Projects

  • Functional Itô Calculus and path-dependent stochastic processes.
  • Convergence analysis of ellipsoid-center methods.
  • Accelerated optimization and momentum methods.
