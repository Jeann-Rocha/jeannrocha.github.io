Projects

Stochastic Calculus

Brownian Motion, Stochastic Calculus and Path Dependence

Study of Brownian motion and stochastic calculus with emphasis on mathematical structures that incorporate dependence on the past trajectory of a stochastic process.

Topics include Itô integration, path-dependent functionals and Functional Itô Calculus.

Optimization

Optimization and Accelerated Methods

Study of optimization methods for smooth and strongly convex functions, including convergence analysis and numerical comparisons between first-order algorithms.

Particular interests include ellipsoid-center methods, gradient descent and accelerated methods.
