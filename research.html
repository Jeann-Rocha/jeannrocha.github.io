My research interests lie at the intersection of analysis, probability, stochastic calculus and optimization.

Current Interests

Stochastic Calculus

Brownian motion, Itô integration, martingales, quadratic variation and stochastic processes.

Functional Itô Calculus

Calculus for functionals defined on spaces of paths, with emphasis on the functional Itô formula and path-dependent functionals.

Path-dependent Systems

Stochastic systems whose evolution depends on the history of a state variable. Applications include mathematical finance and other systems with memory.

Optimization

Smooth and strongly convex optimization, accelerated first-order methods, convergence analysis and numerical experiments.

Projects

Brownian Motion, Stochastic Calculus and Path Dependence

Study of Brownian motion and stochastic calculus with an emphasis on mathematical structures that incorporate dependence on the past trajectory of a stochastic process.

Topics include Itô integration, path-dependent functionals and Functional Itô Calculus.

Optimization and Accelerated Methods

Study of optimization methods for smooth and strongly convex functions, including convergence analysis and numerical comparisons between first-order algorithms.

Particular interests include ellipsoid-center methods, gradient descent and accelerated methods.

Mathematical Interests

More broadly, I am interested in mathematical analysis, probability theory, stochastic processes, functional analysis and optimization.
