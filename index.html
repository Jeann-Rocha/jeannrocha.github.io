I am a mathematics student interested in analysis, probability, stochastic calculus, functional Itô calculus, and optimization.

My work is motivated by the study of mathematical structures that arise in stochastic systems, particularly when the evolution of a system depends not only on its present state, but also on its past.

About

My academic interests lie broadly in mathematics, with an emphasis on analysis, probability and optimization.

I am particularly interested in stochastic calculus and the development of mathematical tools for path-dependent systems. I also study numerical optimization and the analysis of accelerated first-order methods.

This website collects information about my research, academic activities, teaching, and mathematical notes.

Research

My current interests include the following areas:

Stochastic Calculus

Brownian motion, Itô integration, martingales, stochastic processes and stochastic differential equations.

Functional Itô Calculus

Calculus for functionals defined on spaces of paths, with particular interest in the functional Itô formula and path-dependent stochastic systems.

Path-dependent Systems

Mathematical models in which the evolution of a system depends on the history of a state variable rather than only on its current value.

Optimization

Smooth and strongly convex optimization, accelerated methods, convergence analysis and numerical experiments.
